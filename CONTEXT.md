# German vocabulary study

Vocabulary for German exam preparation, organised into two study groups.

## Language

**Word pair**:
One German word or expression and its English meaning, including any accompanying grammatical forms.
_Avoid_: Word, when referring to the whole bilingual entry.

**Study card**:
A German word, expression, or grammatical form paired with its English meaning for study at one stage. A word pair containing material from different stages may become separate study cards.

**Study deck**:
A numbered batch of study cards within a study group.

**A1.1 study group**:
The first group of study cards, guided by chapters 1–6 of Netzwerk neu A1 and including vocabulary that could reasonably be taught in either A1.1 or A1.2.

**A1.2 study group**:
The second group of study cards, guided by chapters 7–12 of Netzwerk neu A1 and containing the remaining vocabulary and forms assigned to later A1 study. Material beyond A1.2 is excluded from both study groups.

**Beyond-A1 material**:
A word, expression, meaning, or grammatical form assessed as later than A1.2. This material is excluded from the study cards even when another part of its original word pair remains useful at A1.
