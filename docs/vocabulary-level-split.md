# Vocabulary level split

## Confirmed scope

The learner approved this plan on 25 October 2026. The goal is preparation for the Goethe-Institut A1.1 course assessment associated with Universität Bremen.

The reference is **Netzwerk neu A1.1 – Kurs- und Übungsbuch mit Audios und Videos** (Klett), using the whole A1.1 volume, **chapters 1–6**. The second stage follows Netzwerk neu A1.2, **chapters 7–12**. A teacher-specific exam cutoff was not supplied; these are study groups based on the agreed whole-book scope.

## Decisions

- Include genuine A1.1/A1.2 borderline vocabulary in **A1.1**, as requested.
- Keep a word and its supplied forms together when they belong to the same study group. Split them only when they belong at different stages. Noun articles, plurals and ordinary supplementary vocabulary remain with their word.
- Put supplied ordinary-verb **Perfekt** forms in **A1.2**, with the correct auxiliary. Do not add a complete tense paradigm where the source supplies none.
- Präteritum of **haben/sein** is part of Netzwerk chapter 6. Other supplied Präteritum forms, such as `arbeitete` and `fuhr ab`, are omitted from this study set.
- Omit material beyond the chosen A1 targets, including modal past forms and further comparison/inflection material. Retain common lexical A1 comparisons and preferences such as `besser`, `am besten`, `lieber` and `mehr`.
- Correct misleading English meanings to the intended everyday A1 sense, with the original wording retained in the audit.
- Absence from a publisher glossary is not sufficient evidence that a word is above A1.2. Use chapter context, the intended meaning and Goethe's A1 references.
- Keep the original source inventory and source numbers for review. Repeated source entries remain identifiable.
- The instruction to omit later material supersedes the earlier requirement to include every complete source pair in the study deck.
