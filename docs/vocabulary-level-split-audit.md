- Original source: **683 pairs**, preserved in [x-flashcards.md](../x-flashcards.md).
- Derived study cards: **706** — **528 A1.1**, **178 A1.2**.
- **23** source entries produce multiple cards; **23** span both stages.
- **50** supplied forms or meanings are omitted from **44** entries. No entire source entry was removed: each has at least one retained A1 word or sense.
- Repeated base words already present in the source are retained with their original source numbers.
- Full machine-readable provenance, original wording and card mappings: [vocabulary-levels.json](../anki/vocabulary-levels.json).
